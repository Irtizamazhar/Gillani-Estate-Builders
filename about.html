Our story

From a small office to Islamabad's trusted builders.

Founded by Syed Zeeshan Kazmi, Gillani Estate started as a property consultancy in Islamabad. As clients asked us to build what they bought, we grew into Gillani Builders — a full-service developer delivering apartments, villas and commercial centres.

Today our team of consultants, engineers and craftsmen manages every step: finding the right property, designing it, building it and handing over the keys.
